# The story of Ultima-J: The Abyss Below

*[Polska wersja](STORY.pl.md)*

> This is the whole story, both endings included. If you want to find it out
> by playing, stop here.

## Before the game

Long ago Lord Varian founded a colony deep in the great Abyss. Men, the
mountain folk, goblins and lizardmen lived there as one, bound together by the
light of the Lumen Stone. Varian's sworn shields were the Knights of the Lumen.

Malchor, the court mage, wanted the Stone's power for himself. One night he
struck at it. One of Varian's knights threw themselves between the mage and
their lord. The blast shattered the Stone into three shards, Valor, Wisdom and
Mercy, and killed the knight. Their friend Sir Aldric buried them in a shallow
grave by the great doors at the top of the Abyss.

Without the Stone the colony fell apart. The peoples turned on each other, and
the great doors sealed themselves. Since then nobody living has left the Abyss.
Varian couldn't mend the Stone. He went down below the crypts, sat on the
Throne of Souls and has held the dead of the Abyss there ever since, so that
no soul would wander. Behind him he sealed the way to his throne with a gate
that opens only to the light of the Stone, that is, to all three shards
together. The shards went to the peoples who could guard them: Valor to the
mountain folk, Wisdom to the lizardmen, and Mercy to the dead, kept by Sir
Aldric in the crypts.

The blast didn't kill Malchor either. It ruined his body, and he has kept
himself alive by drinking souls. He wants the throne, but the gate's light
burns him, and none of the peoples would ever give their shard to the man who
broke the Stone.

Now, years later, Varian is old and weak. The dead slip out of his grasp and
crawl back into their bones as hollows: empty, mindless and hungry.

## The game

**The awakening.** You claw your way out of the grave by the great doors, grey
and cold, one of the risen dead. Unlike the hollows, you can still think, but
you remember nothing. A voice from far below calls you "my knight", says it is
the Lord of Souls and promises you your life if you bring it the three shards.

**The Upper Halls.** Elsmore, a scholar trapped among the outcasts, tells you
about the colony, the shards and the Lord of Souls, and that his throne lies
behind a gate only the shards can open. Tobin trades in whatever the dead no
longer need. The way down is held by the gray goblins. Their chief, Rhugg,
opens the gate to the stairs if you bring him the medallion of Gnarl, chief of
the rival green goblins, or pay him in gold.

**The Mines of the Mountain Folk.** Thorgrim, King under the Mountain, keeps
the Shard of Valor. Giant spiders have taken the eastern galleries, where the
Hammer of the Mountain, his family's heirloom, still lies. When you bring the
Hammer back, he gives you the shard. Hilda feeds you, and Bodric the smith
trades and mends your gear.

**The Flooded Warrens.** The lizardmen's village is mourning a clutch of eggs
stolen by the lurkers of the deep water. When you return the eggs, Ssrath, the
Sage of the People, gives you the Shard of Wisdom. He also warns you that
another sorcerer, Malchor, passed this way hungry for the Lord's throne.

**The Crypts.** The ghost of Sir Aldric can't rest, because trolls have
dragged his bones out of his tomb. When you bring them back, he gives you the
Shard of Mercy. He also recognises you. You were a Knight of the Lumen, his
brother in arms, and you died shielding Varian from Malchor's blast. When you
tell him that a voice woke you, he warns you that Malchor has a silver tongue
and knows the dead better than any living man. In the crypts you also find a
silver sapling, which calls you back if you fall.

**The Deep Sanctum.** Malchor's thralls guard the last level: hollow souls he
snatched before Varian could take them. His study holds his notes, and a
careful reader can see the plan in them: *wake the knight who took my blast,
speak to it in Varian's voice, and wait by the gate.*

**The reveal.** When you lay the three shards on the gate, it opens, and
Malchor steps out behind you. The voice in your grave and in your dreams was
his. Varian has had no strength to call anyone for years. Malchor couldn't
pass the gate or win the shards himself, so he raised one of Varian's own
knights to fetch them. He demands the shards, and you fight. When he dies,
his thralls crumble with him.

**The Throne of Souls.** Varian, a gaunt, crowned king among the lava pools,
knows you at once. He never called you, and he is sorry that Malchor used you.
Then he lays out the choice.

## Side quests

The people of the Abyss have their own troubles. None of these is needed to
finish the game.

**The Chronicle of the Colony.** Maera, the colony's chronicler, came down
with Varian's first three hundred and wrote everything down. Her pages lie
scattered across all five levels, one on each: in her hidden study off the
library, in an old miners' vein behind a secret door, in an empty grave in the
lizardmen's ossuary, in a cell in the crypts, and with her body in the cave of
fire, where she died after following Varian down. Her brother Elsmore came
into the Abyss to find her; he says he came to study the ruins. He pays for
each page, and for the last one he gives up his savings and what he knows of
lore. Read in order, the pages tell the founding, the four peoples living as
one, Malchor's hunger for the Stone, the night it broke (the knight at
Varian's left hand who threw themselves into the blast), and Maera's last
words: remember the dead by their names.

**The Upper Halls.** Corwin, captain of the colony guard, wrote the words by
the great doors and has been drunk ever since. On the night the Stone broke he
should have stood at Varian's left hand, but he was drinking in the guard
hall, and a young knight stood in his place and died. He doesn't recognise
you. The green goblins stole his sword; bring it back from their hoard and he
teaches you how a guard fights. Tobin and his partner Wenna kept the colony's
stores before the fall and went on trading afterwards. Wenna went looking for
an old cache in the bat roost and never came back; her ring is on her body,
and Tobin gives you her lantern for it. The guard hall's armoury opens to a
lock of three levers, set as Corwin's plaque says the watch should stand.

**The Mines of the Mountain Folk.** Hilda's son Brann jumped for the gold on
the ledge in the old diggings and broke his leg; a healing potion gets him
home, and Hilda gives you his father's armour. The body in the flooded drift
is Dain, Bodric's apprentice, who was sent for clay before the king sealed the
spider galleries with him inside. Bring Bodric his hammer and he forges you a
weapon. The treasury under the storeroom opens to three levers, set as a
miner works the face.

**The Flooded Warrens.** The clutch-mother Ssiva is sick with grief for her
stolen eggs, and Hissk asks for three mushrooms to rouse her. Ssethra, the
lone lizardman by the stairs down, was exiled for saying the People should
trade with the warm-bloods; carry his plea to Ssrath, who forgives him, and he
goes home. The sunken vault opens to plates at the bottom of a flooded
sinkhole: who would rise with the treasure must first go down.

**The Crypts.** Ysolde, the last embalmer, still haunts her rooms. The hollows
carried her book of rites into the catacombs; with it she lays the dead of the
mass graves to rest, and a note in its margin shows where Maera's cell is.
Aldric's squire Pell died trying to stop the trolls, and they threw him into
the mass graves; Aldric asks for his shield, so that someone remembers him.
The tomb of the three kings opens to the levers of the merciful kings.

**The Deep Sanctum.** Ilsa, Malchor's apprentice, is kept in the cells to
grind bone-dust and stir his cauldron. She tells you who Malchor was, and for
her notebook she gives you the copy she made of his vault key. In the vault
are the souls Malchor jarred; Varian takes them back into his keeping. The
notebook also gives away Malchor's private way: a lever by his cauldron
raises a bridge over the lava to his hoard.

## The endings

**Live again.** You give Varian the shards. The Stone is life, and he kindles
the flame in your chest once more. Your heart beats, and far above the great
doors grind open. You climb out into the daylight alive. Behind you, in the
dark, the old lord keeps holding the dead, his flame burning low.

**Strike him down.** Varian tells you about the oldest law of the dead, as it
was told to him: the Throne of Souls is not given, it is taken. Only the light
of the shards can hurt him. When he falls, you sit on the throne and wait for
its power, but nothing comes. The shards go dark in your hand. There was no
law, only an old man's will, and it is spent.

The seal on the great doors was Varian's, and it dies with him. For the first
time since the Stone broke, daylight falls into the Abyss. The Stone is dead,
the doors are open and no one rules the Abyss. The mountain folk march up from
the mines, the lizardmen rise from the warrens, and the goblins hold the upper
halls. Old neighbours kill each other in the ruins of the colony. At last the
three peoples meet at the great doors, weapons drawn, in the first daylight
any of them has seen. Whoever holds the doorway will take the world above, and
none of them will let the others go first.
