# Polish translation of Ultima-J. See src/lang.rs for the format.
#
# Phrases: an English line exactly as in the code, then "= " and the Polish
# line. {} and {N} are filled in by the game; in Polish a noun may take a
# case, {:acc} or {:gen} ({:Nom} also capitalises it), {a|b} gives the male
# and female forms for the player, and {N?m|f|n|p} picks by the gender of
# noun N. \n is a line break.
#
# Nouns: @english = nominative | genitive | accusative | nominative plural |
# genitive plural | gender (m, ma = animate, mp = personal, f, n, p = plural
# only). Adjectives are kept in the masculine nominative and inflected by the
# game (a word starting with _ is left as it is).

# ------------------------------------------------------------- help

MOVING: W run, S walk, X back, A/D turn, Z/C sidestep (or the arrow keys). Hold the left mouse button in the view: the cursor arrow shows the direction, and further from the centre is faster. J or Space jumps. 1/3 look down/up, 2 centres the view. E/Q fly up/down when flying.\n\nCOMMANDS: click an icon on the left (or F2 Talk, F3 Get, F4 Look, F5 Fight, F6 Use), then right-click things in the view. With no icon selected, right-click looks; hold and drag the right button to get, use or talk.\n\nINVENTORY: left-click picks items up and puts them down. Drop armour on your body, weapons and lights in the hand and shoulder circles. Right-click an item to use it: eat, drink, read, light a torch, open a bag, repair worn weapons and armour. Left-click the rune bag to see your runes. Click the chain (or F7) for your statistics; spend skill points there after gaining a level.\n\nBARTER: when a trader shows you their goods, click the ones you want (top left of the conversation), put gold or items from your inventory in your own trade area (top right), and choose Let us trade. Right-click either side to ask what something is worth.\n\nFIGHTING: select Fight (F5), aim, hold the right button to draw back and watch the power gem, release to strike. High in the view bashes, middle slashes, low thrusts (or P, ; and . keys).\n\nMAGIC: click runes in the rune bag to put them on the shelf, then click the shelf (or F8) to cast. Some spells ask for a target. Right-click the shelf to clear it.\n\nOTHER: M or Tab opens the map. F9 tracks, F10 sleeps. Use a trap you have spotted to try to disarm it. Click the compass for your condition, the flasks for vitality and mana. Ctrl+S quick-saves, Ctrl+R restores, F1/Esc opens the options. F11 toggles fullscreen, F12 toggles the 4:3 aspect.\n\nThe options menu switches to modern controls.

# --------------------------------------------------- intro and ending

Awaken, my knight. I am the Lord of Souls, and I speak to thee from beyond the veil of death...
= Przebudź się, mój rycerzu. Jestem Pan Dusz i przemawiam do ciebie zza zasłony śmierci...
Long ago, Lord Varian founded a colony deep within the great Abyss, where all peoples might live as one, bound by the light of the Lumen Stone.
= Dawno temu lord Varian założył kolonię głęboko w wielkiej Otchłani, gdzie wszystkie ludy mogły żyć razem, złączone światłem Kamienia Lumen.
Then the Stone was broken into three shards, and the colony fell into ruin and war. Varian went down to the Throne of Souls, to keep the dead of the Abyss at rest.
= Potem Kamień rozpadł się na trzy odłamki, a kolonia pogrążyła się w ruinie i wojnie. Varian zszedł do Tronu Dusz, by umarli Otchłani zaznali spokoju.
But the Lord of Souls grows weak. The dead no longer rest. One by one they rise from their graves - hollow, nameless, lost.
= Lecz Pan Dusz słabnie. Umarli nie zaznają już spokoju. Jeden po drugim wstają z grobów - wydrążeni, bezimienni, zagubieni.
You open your eyes in cold earth. You remember nothing: not your name, not your death. Only a voice, calling from far below.
= Otwierasz oczy w zimnej ziemi. Nie pamiętasz niczego: ani swojego imienia, ani swojej śmierci. Tylko głos, który woła z głębin.
The great doors above are sealed. "Bring me the three shards of the Lumen Stone," the voice whispers, "and I shall give thee back thy life." The Abyss is waiting...
= Wielkie Wrota w górze są zamknięte. "Przynieś mi trzy odłamki Kamienia Lumen", szepcze głos, "a oddam ci życie". Otchłań czeka...
The Lord of Souls falls. His crown rings once upon the stone. "So be it, my knight," he breathes, and the old king is still.
= Pan Dusz pada. Jego korona raz dźwięczy o kamień. "Niech tak będzie, mój rycerzu", szepcze, i stary król nieruchomieje.
You climb onto the Throne of Souls and wait for its power. Nothing comes. The shards flicker in your hand and go dark. There was no law - only an old man's will, and it is spent.
= Siadasz na Tronie Dusz i czekasz na jego moc. Nic się nie dzieje. Odłamki migoczą ci w dłoni i gasną. Nie było żadnego prawa - była tylko wola starego człowieka, a ta się wyczerpała.
Far above, the great doors groan. Their seal was Varian's, and it has died with him. For the first time since the Stone broke, daylight falls into the Abyss.
= Daleko w górze jęczą Wielkie Wrota. Ich pieczęć była dziełem Variana i umarła razem z nim. Po raz pierwszy od rozbicia Kamienia do Otchłani wpada światło dnia.
The Stone is dead, the doors are open, and no one rules the Abyss. The mountain folk march up from the mines, the lizardmen rise from the warrens, the goblins hold the upper halls. Old neighbours kill each other in the ruins of the colony.
= Kamień jest martwy, wrota stoją otworem i nikt nie rządzi Otchłanią. Górski lud maszeruje w górę z kopalń, jaszczuroludzie wychodzą z nor, a gobliny trzymają Górne Sale. Dawni sąsiedzi zabijają się nawzajem w ruinach kolonii.
At the great doors the three peoples meet, weapons drawn, in the first daylight any of them has seen. Whoever holds the doorway will take the world above - and none of them will let the others go first.
= Przy Wielkich Wrotach spotykają się trzy ludy, z bronią w ręku, w pierwszym świetle dnia, jakie którykolwiek z nich widział. Kto utrzyma przejście, ten weźmie świat na górze - a żaden z nich nie przepuści pozostałych przodem.
