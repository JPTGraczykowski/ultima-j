=== elsmore
-- start
Another poor soul thrown into the pit! Welcome, if welcome is the word. I am Elsmore, once a scholar of the Baron's court, now just another outcast warming his hands at this miserable fire.
* Who art thou? -> who
* What is this place? -> place
* I am looking for the Baron's daughter. -> daughter ? !flag:elsmore_daughter
* Tell me again about the girl. -> daughter2 ? flag:elsmore_daughter
* Have you any food to spare? -> food ? !flag:elsmore_food
* How does magic work down here? -> magic
* Farewell. -> end

-- who
I came down here years ago to study the ruins of Lord Varian's colony. The Baron's men sealed the gate behind me "for my own safety". There is no way out the way thou camest, {name}.
* What happened to the colony? -> place
* Back to other matters. -> start

-- place
This is the Abyss. Lord Varian built a colony here where men, mountain folk, goblins and lizardmen lived as one, bound by the light of the Lumen Stone. When he died, the Stone was broken into three shards and the peoples turned on each other.
* Where are the shards now? -> shards
* Back to other matters. -> start

-- shards
Valor went to the mountain folk, one level below. Wisdom to the lizardmen of the flooded warrens. Mercy lies with the dead in the crypts. Or so the old tales say.
! flag know_shards 1
* Thank thee. -> start

-- daughter
Lady Rowena? Aye, I heard screams some nights ago, and saw a robed man drag a girl past this very fire toward the stairs. A wizard, by his look. He went down, deep down. The way down lies beyond the hall of the gray goblins, to the north-east.
! flag elsmore_daughter 1
! exp 20
* How do I get past the gray goblins? -> goblins
* Back to other matters. -> start

-- daughter2
The robed man took her down the stairs beyond the gray goblins' hall. Find their chief, Rhugg. He can be reasoned with, unlike the green brutes to the west.
* How do I get past the gray goblins? -> goblins
* Back to other matters. -> start

-- goblins
Rhugg guards the portcullis to the stairs. He hates Gnarl, chief of the green goblins in the den beyond the west door, more than he hates us. Bring him proof of Gnarl's death and he may let thee pass. The green goblins also stole the key to our library - north of this fire.
! flag know_rhugg 1
* Thank thee, Elsmore. -> start

-- magic
Magic in the Abyss is worked with rune stones. Place them in thy rune bag, then choose up to three upon thy shelf and cast. IN LOR makes light - very handy down here. ORT JUX hurls a bolt of force. Thou wilt need to be experienced to cast the mightier spells.
* Back to other matters. -> start

-- food
Hmm. Thou hast an honest face. Take this loaf - and do not tell the others.
! give Bread
! flag elsmore_food 1
* Thank thee. -> start
