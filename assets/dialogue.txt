=== elsmore
-- start
Gods preserve us - another corpse crawled out of the old graves! Stay back! ... Wait. Thou speakest? Then thou art not hollow yet. I am Elsmore, once a scholar of the surface, now just another outcast warming his hands at this miserable fire.
* Who art thou? -> who
* Who am I? -> self
* How do I get out of here? -> out ? !flag:elsmore_out
* Tell me again of the way down. -> out2 ? flag:elsmore_out
* Have you any food to spare? -> food ? !flag:elsmore_food
* Farewell. -> end

-- who
I came down here years ago to study the ruins of Lord Varian's colony. Then the great doors sealed themselves behind me, as they have sealed behind every soul since. The living do not leave the Abyss, {name}. Nor, these days, do the dead stay dead.
* What happened to the colony? -> place
* How does magic work down here? -> magic
* Back to other matters. -> start

-- place
This is the Abyss. Lord Varian built a colony here where men, mountain folk, goblins and lizardmen lived as one, bound by the light of the Lumen Stone. One night the Stone was broken into three shards, and the peoples turned on each other.
* Where are the shards now? -> shards
* What became of Lord Varian? -> varian
* Back to other matters. -> start

-- shards
Valor went to the mountain folk, one level below. Wisdom to the lizardmen of the flooded warrens. Mercy lies with the dead in the crypts. Or so the old tales say.
! flag know_shards 1
* Thank thee. -> start

-- varian
The tales say he did not die. He went down, below even the crypts, and sat upon a throne of the dead to keep their souls from wandering. The Lord of Souls, the goblins call him. But something is wrong down there. The dead walk again - more of them every year - and most of them have no more mind than a beetle.
! flag know_lord 1
* Back to other matters. -> start

-- self
How should I know? Thou climbed out of a grave by the sealed doors, grey as ash. Thou wert buried a long time ago, by the look of thy gear. Most who rise like that are hollow - empty, mindless, hungry. Thou art... different. Keep thy wits about thee, or thou'lt lose them.
* Back to other matters. -> start

-- out
Out? Ha! The great doors have not opened since the Stone broke. Only the Lord of Souls holds their key - and only he, they say, can give a dead man back his life. But his throne lies behind a gate sealed by the light of the Stone, and only the three shards together will open it. If thou wouldst find him, go down. The way down lies beyond the hall of the gray goblins, to the north-east.
! flag elsmore_out 1
! exp 20
* How do I get past the gray goblins? -> goblins
* Back to other matters. -> start

-- out2
The stairs down lie beyond the gray goblins' hall. Find their chief, Rhugg. He can be reasoned with, unlike the green brutes to the west.
* How do I get past the gray goblins? -> goblins
* Back to other matters. -> start

-- goblins
Rhugg guards the portcullis to the stairs. He hates Gnarl, chief of the green goblins in the den beyond the west door, more than he hates us. Bring him proof of Gnarl's death and he may let thee pass. The green goblins also stole the key to our library - north of this fire.
! flag know_rhugg 1
* Thank thee, Elsmore. -> start

-- magic
Magic in the Abyss is worked with rune stones. Place them in thy rune bag, then choose up to three upon thy shelf and cast. IN LOR makes light - very handy down here. ORT JUX hurls a bolt of force. Thou wilt need to be experienced to cast the mightier spells.
* Back to other matters. -> start

-- food
Even the dead get hungry down here, it seems. Take this loaf - and do not tell the others.
! give Bread
! flag elsmore_food 1
* Thank thee. -> start

=== malchor
-- start

* Thou art the one who broke the Lumen Stone. -> broke

-- voice
Mine in thy grave, and mine in thy dreams. I wore his crown in them and called thee my knight, and thou didst crawl all the way down with the shards, as loyal in death as in life. The dead are so very trusting.
* Why didst thou need me? -> why
* I will never give thee the shards. -> fight

-- why
The shards are the Lumen Stone, and the Stone sealed this gate behind Varian. Its light remembers what I did, and it burns me. But it would open for thee, a Knight of the Lumen who died for it. The peoples would never give their shards to me. To thee they gave them gladly.
* And now thou wouldst take them. -> shards
* Thou art the one who broke the Lumen Stone. -> broke

-- shards
Give them to me and go. I shall take the throne, and thou mayst rot wherever thou likest.
* Never! -> fight

-- thralls
My thralls. Hollow souls I snatched before Varian could take them. They walk on my power, and they will walk as long as I do. Thou couldst have been one of them, knight. Thou still mayst.
* Never! -> fight
* Why didst thou need me? -> why

-- broke
Broke it? I freed it! Varian hoarded that light like a miser. I would have made us gods. Thy soul is old and strong, knight. It will make a fine meal.

=== lord
-- start

-- what
Once I was Varian, lord of a colony where all peoples lived as one. When the Stone broke, I came down here and took up the Throne of Souls, so that the dead of the Abyss might rest. I have held them ever since. But I am old, {name}, and my flame gutters.

* Back. -> start

Strike me down with the light of the shards and sit in my place - never living, never dying, lord of every soul that falls in the dark. Choose, my knight. I will not blame thee either way.
* Then I will take thy throne. -> throne ? item:ShardOfValor & item:ShardOfWisdom & item:ShardOfMercy
